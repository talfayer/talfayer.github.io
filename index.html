About

My interest in cybersecurity comes from a lifelong pull toward two things: mathematics and global affairs. When I came to school I was torn between the two — until I realized cybersecurity is where they actually meet. It lets me use analytical, quantitative skills while staying rooted in a human-centered field, one where the work has real consequences for people and institutions rather than existing in the abstract. That combination — rigorous problem-solving in service of something beyond myself — is what keeps me drawn to it.
